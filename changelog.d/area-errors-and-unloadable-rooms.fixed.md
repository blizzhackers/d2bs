- **Area errors and rooms the game cannot load.** An `Area`'s properties wait while an area or act loads, and `getPath()` and an `Area`'s properties report an area outside the player's act as an error a script can catch, as `getCollision()` does; `getCollision()`, `area.exits` and `getPath()` reduction types 0 to 2 treat a room the game cannot load as blocked.
