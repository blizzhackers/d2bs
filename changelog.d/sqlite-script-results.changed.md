- **Clearer SQLite statement results.** `SQLite.query()` and `DBStatement.bind()` bind `true` and `false` as the integers 1 and 0 and `null` as NULL, and throw an error the script can catch for a parameter number out of range (including extra `query()` arguments) or a `bind()` after a `next()` or a `skip()` of 1 or more without `reset()`. Every DBStatement method except `close()` throws "The statement is closed" once the statement is finished by `close()`, `go()` or its database's `close()`, and such a statement's `sql` reads undefined. `db.statements` lists only the statements that are still open, from index 0, and `skip(n)` with a count of 0 or less skips nothing and returns 0, while a `next()`, `reset()` or `go()` that reports an error leaves the statement not ready, and a `next()` or `skip()` called after the results end starts again from the first row.
